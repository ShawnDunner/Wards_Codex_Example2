Frequently Asked Questions

What is included in a safety certificate inspection?
A safety certificate inspection checks critical roadworthy components such as tyres, brakes, steering, suspension, lights, windscreen, and body condition to ensure the vehicle meets Queensland safety standards.
Why is logbook servicing important?
Logbook servicing follows the manufacturer schedule, helps protect your warranty, catches wear issues early, and keeps your vehicle running efficiently.
How often should I have my car serviced?
Most vehicles should be serviced every 10,000 km or every 6 months, whichever comes first. Always check your owner manual for exact intervals.
Do you service all makes and models of vehicles?
Yes. We service a wide range of makes and models, including petrol and diesel vehicles. Contact us with your vehicle details and we can confirm availability.
How long does a standard service take?
A standard service is usually completed within a few hours, depending on vehicle type and condition. We will provide a realistic timeframe at booking.
Do you offer a warranty on your repairs?
Yes. We stand behind our workmanship and parts. Specific warranty terms may vary by repair type and parts supplier.
What payment methods do you accept?
We accept major cards, EFTPOS, and cash. If you need another option, ask our team when booking.
Can I wait at the workshop while my car is being serviced?
Yes, short services can often be waited on. For larger jobs we recommend drop-off and we can call when your vehicle is ready.
What should I do if my car breaks down and I need emergency assistance?
Move to a safe location if possible, turn on hazard lights, and contact roadside assistance. Then submit a booking enquiry and we can guide your next steps.
